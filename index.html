How a turn works
  1. Your opponent flips one card from its deck (more, if it holds Extra RAM). A gem card scores a point; the deck reshuffles at the start of its next turn.
  2. You either advance the algorithm (move one tile right) or measure (resolve the odds on your current tile). Measuring always returns the arrow to tile 1.
  3. If events are on, a card flips and may affect either side — Decoherence blocks a tile, Cosmic Ray forces your next measurement, Extra RAM speeds up the opponent, Blue Screen resets its deck, Quantum Error Correction shields you from the next Decoherence.
  4. First to the gem target wins.
